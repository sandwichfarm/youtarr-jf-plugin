Youtarr Metadata

Youtarr Metadata organizes your Youtarr download folder into a Jellyfin TV Shows library: each YouTube channel folder becomes a Series and each video becomes an Episode.

When enabled, episodes are grouped by upload year (e.g. Season 2024). When disabled, all episodes are placed in a single Season 1.
YYYYMMDD encodes the upload date as the episode number. Note: two videos uploaded on the same day will share an episode number.
YouTube descriptions are often very long. Set 0 for no truncation. Default: 500 characters.
